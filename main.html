1. The Protective Layer: Your request first hits the Cloudflare Global Edge. I have enforced DNSSEC, HSTS, and WAF rules to prevent spoofing and bot scraping before traffic ever touches my infrastructure.

2. The Stealth Bridge: Most servers have "Open Ports" (80/443). Mine has Zero Inbound Ports. Instead, a cloudflared daemon creates an outbound connection to the edge. Traffic flows down this secure pipe directly to the container.

3. The Hardened Payload: The site runs inside a Dockerized Nginx-Alpine container. I've injected apk upgrade into the build process to remediate upstream OS vulnerabilities at the image level.

Architectural Logic

Why Terraform?
Manual clicks are errors waiting to happen. I use Terraform with a GCS Remote Backend. This ensures my infrastructure is version-controlled, idempotent, and recoverable in seconds.
Why Systemd GitOps?
A GCP e2-micro has only 1GB RAM. Traditional CI/CD runners (Jenkins/GitHub Actions) would trigger an OOM crash. I wrote a native Bash + Systemd poller to handle GitOps with near-zero overhead.
